Technology

M‑Cantronic. More power – without touching the factory software.

The MTM add-on control unit optimises boost-pressure and injection sensor signals in real time via the CAN bus. The engine software stays untouched and the car can be returned to stock at any time.

Questions & answers

What customers want to know before the build.

Can the power upgrade be registered?

Yes. We supply the documents needed for registration with our power stages and components. For custom builds we clarify registration beforehand.

What happens to the manufacturer warranty?

Modifying the engine or control units can limit the manufacturer warranty for affected parts. We discuss this openly before the build. Our components and work are covered by the MTM warranty.

How long does a build take?

Software update or M-Cantronic: a few hours. Suspension, brakes or exhaust: one to two workshop days. An engine build takes considerably longer – we state the timeframe in the quote.

Can I return the car to stock?

The M-Cantronic can be removed at any time without a trace. Suspension, exhaust and wheels are reversible. Reverting an engine build is more involved – we advise you in advance.

Do I have to bring my car to Wettstetten?

For installation and dyno calibration, yes. Individual components such as wheels or exhausts can also be fitted by partner workshops.
